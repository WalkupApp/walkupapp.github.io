Spotify Setup

Use Spotify for your walk-up songs on game day

⚡

Spotify Premium required. Spotify's free tier doesn't allow remote playback control, which WalkUp Songs uses to play music through your speaker.

How It Works

Because of how iOS handles background apps, Spotify playback works best with two devices — one running WalkUp Songs and another running Spotify, both connected to the same speaker.

WalkUp Songs controls Spotify remotely — picking the right song, starting at the right offset, and fading out when it's time. The second device just needs Spotify open and connected.

Setup Steps

  1. Connect your speaker to the device that will run Spotify (Device 2) via Bluetooth or AirPlay.
  2. Open Spotify on Device 2 and play any song briefly to make sure audio goes to the speaker. You can pause it after.
  3. On your main device (Device 1), open WalkUp Songs → Settings and turn on Spotify. Log in when prompted.
  4. Pick songs for your players from the Spotify library. Set the start offset so each song begins at the perfect moment.
  5. On game day, make sure Spotify is open and connected on Device 2 before heading into Play Ball mode.

Tip: An old phone, iPad, or iPod touch works great as Device 2. It just needs Spotify installed, a Wi-Fi or cellular connection, and Bluetooth for the speaker.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need two devices?
iOS suspends apps that aren't in the foreground. If Spotify and WalkUp Songs are on the same device, Spotify loses its connection to the speaker when WalkUp Songs is active. A second device keeps Spotify running and connected.
What if I only have one device?
You can still use Apple Music songs, which play directly on the device without needing a second phone. Spotify requires the two-device setup for reliable playback.
Does it work with Bluetooth speakers?
Yes! Bluetooth speakers, AirPlay speakers, and wired speakers all work. Just make sure Device 2 (running Spotify) is the one connected to the speaker.
Do both devices need internet?
Yes. Device 1 needs internet to send playback commands to Spotify, and Device 2 needs internet to stream the music. Wi-Fi or cellular both work.
Why does it say "No active device found"?
This means Spotify isn't active on Device 2. Open Spotify, play a song briefly so it registers as a device, and try again from WalkUp Songs.
Can I use a computer for Device 2?
Yes! The Spotify desktop app on a Mac or PC works great as Device 2. Just make sure it's connected to your speaker and Spotify is open.
