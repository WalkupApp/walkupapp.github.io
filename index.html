How it works

  1. Build your lineup — add players with their name, number, and position.
  2. Pick a walk-up song for each player from Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube, or your own files.
  3. On game day, tap a player's name to instantly play their walk-up song through your speaker.
  4. Add AI announcer intros, soundboard effects, and inning-break DJ sets to complete the experience.

Why WalkUp Songs?

Every kid deserves a big-league moment stepping up to the plate. WalkUp Songs makes it easy for coaches and parents to set up personalized walk-up music and announcer intros for every player on the team — no DJ experience required.

Whether you're running a youth baseball league, a travel softball team, or a rec league, WalkUp Songs turns game day into an event. Pick from Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube, or local audio files — whatever your players love.

Beyond walk-up songs, add AI-generated announcer intros, a soundboard for crowd effects, and curated inning-break DJ sets to keep the energy high from first pitch to last out.
