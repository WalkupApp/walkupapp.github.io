WalkUp Songs — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 9, 2026

WalkUp Songs ("the App") is a walk-up song player for youth baseball and softball teams. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is stored, and what is shared with third parties.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not require account creation, does not track you, and does not use analytics or advertising services.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Team & player names — names you enter for teams and players.
  • Song selections — references to songs you pick from Apple Music or text-to-speech phrases.
  • App preferences — settings such as playback duration, stored in UserDefaults.

This data never leaves your device except through iCloud sync (see below).

3. iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud in the App's settings, your team and player data is synced via iCloud Documents to your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable iCloud sync at any time in the App's settings.

4. Apple Music & MusicKit

The App uses Apple's MusicKit framework to let you search for and play songs from Apple Music. Song searches and playback are handled entirely by Apple's services. We do not access your Apple Music listening history or library.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for use by coaches, parents, and families. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at walkupapp@gmail.com.
