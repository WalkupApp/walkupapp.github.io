Share a Team

Missing a game and someone else has to run the music? Hand them your lineup.

📣
Works the same idea on iPhone and Android, including sharing from one to the other. Send them a copy so they can hit the ground running.
⭐
Premium features on a shared team need Premium on the receiving phone. Anyone can send the link. The coach who imports the team needs WalkUp Songs Premium to use the Premium features that came with that copy.

How to share

On the phone that already has the team

  • Open WalkUp Songs and open the team.
  • Tap Share (or the share icon on the team).
  • Send the link by text, email, or AirDrop — or have them scan the QR code on your screen.

On the phone that will run the game

  • Install WalkUp Songs from the App Store or Google Play if it isn’t there yet.
  • Open the link (or scan the QR).
  • Tap Open in WalkUp Songs.
  • The team imports as its own copy. Open it and tap through a couple of players before first pitch.
  • If a feature on that copy is marked Premium, subscribe on this phone to use it.
Not the same as iCloud. iCloud only syncs your own devices on the same Apple ID. Sharing is how a different coach — or an Android phone — gets the roster.

Planning and testing ahead of time is always suggested.

What actually comes across

The link carries the lineup: names, numbers, order, and which song each player is set to. It does not copy every audio file off your phone. Plan for these limits:

Local files

The MP3 itself is not inside the share link. Send the song files separately (AirDrop, Google Drive, text, or a shared folder), then the other coach imports them on their phone. See the Local Files Guide. On iPhone, mark them Keep Downloaded so iCloud doesn’t offload them at the field.

Apple Music catalog

A catalog song can show up on the other phone, but they need their own Apple Music access to play it. If their account can’t play that track, reassign it or switch that player to a local file.

Your music library

Songs that only exist in your library — ripped CDs, purchases synced to your phone, personal uploads — do not play on someone else’s device. Those players need a local file (or a catalog song both people can stream).

YouTube

A YouTube pick may open on the other phone, but it needs a working internet connection at the field. For a substitute coach, local files are the more reliable handoff.

Best handoff: use local files for the walk-up songs, share the team link, and send the same MP3 folder. Then game day doesn’t depend on anyone’s Apple Music account or cell service.

Before you hand off the phone duties

🔁
It’s a snapshot, not a live link If you change the lineup after sharing, the other phone does not update. Share again and have them import the new copy.
🔊
They bring the speaker connection Bluetooth pairs to the phone that’s playing. The substitute should connect the speaker to their phone and do a volume check.
⌚
Apple Watch stays with its phone Watch controls follow the phone the watch is paired to. Sharing the team does not share your watch.
👤
One player is fine too You can share a single player the same way if someone only needs one walk-up song, not the whole roster.
