How to cancel WalkUp Songs purchases managed by Apple or Google

ℹ️
Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside WalkUp Songs. Cancel through the App Store on iPhone/iPad or Google Play on Android. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
iPhone / iPad Cancel in Apple Settings → Subscriptions Android Cancel in the Google Play Store app

Cancel on iPhone or iPad

Use the Apple ID that purchased the subscription.

  • Open the Settings app.
  • Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  • Tap WalkUp Songs (or the subscription you want to cancel).
  • Tap Cancel Subscription. You may need to scroll down to find it.
  • Confirm the cancellation when prompted.
Already canceled? If there’s no Cancel button — or you see a red expiration message — the subscription is already set to end and won’t renew.

You can also manage subscriptions in the App Store: tap your profile photo → Subscriptions.

Official Apple help: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Cancel on Android

Use the Google account that purchased the subscription.

  • Open the Google Play Store app.
  • Tap your profile icon (top right).
  • Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Select WalkUp Songs.
  • Tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.
Important: Deleting the app from your phone does not stop billing. You must cancel in Google Play.

Official Google help: Cancel a subscription on Google Play.

What happens after you cancel

📅
Access continues until the period ends You’ll usually keep premium features until the end of the current billing period. You won’t be charged again after that.
🧾
One-time purchases don’t renew Things like Remove Ads are one-time purchases — there’s nothing to cancel. They stay on your account.
🎵
Apple Music / Spotify are separate Those are billed by Apple or Spotify, not as a WalkUp Songs subscription. Cancel them in their own account settings if needed.
